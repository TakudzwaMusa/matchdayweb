Request Account Deletion

⚠️ Important Information

Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data, including:

  • Your profile information and settings
  • All posts, comments, and content you've created
  • Your points, rewards, and achievements
  • Your Fandom memberships and interactions

Account deletion takes 48 days to fully process.

During this 48-day period, your account will be deactivated and you will not be able to log in. You may cancel the deletion request by contacting support within this period.

Changed your mind? Contact support to cancel your deletion request within the 48-day period.
