Matchday Superfan is the ultimate social hub where fans connect, share, and celebrate their passion. Built by Matchday Technologies, the platform enhances matchday experiences while driving engagement, rewards, and community growth through a freemium model.

Welcome to the future

Intro

The Matchday SuperFan App is designed to promote stadium attendances by enhancing the fan’s match day experience and fundraising activities. The SuperFan App is rewards based, and operated on a ‘Freemium’ business model. This revolutionary App is an all encompassing tool which enhances the user’s match day experience through exciting features and provides the user with a user-profile and memorabilia with each event attended.

Company Background

Matchday Technologies develops digital platforms for sporting events, specializing in mobile applications and software solutions that enhance fan engagement, event management, and commercial partnerships.
