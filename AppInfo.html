Matchday Superfan

App Information & Platform Overview

What is Matchday Superfan?

Matchday Superfan is a social media platform designed to bring home-viewing fans (SuperFans) as close as possible to the real in-stadium experience. Whether you're cheering from your couch or from the stands, the app makes sure you feel connected, engaged, and part of the action.

For SuperFans at home, it's about contributing to your team's journey from anywhere in the world, while still enjoying the thrill and energy of the stadium. For fans attending live matches, it's about enhancing that stadium experience with premium content features – creating a virtual stadium that others can join.

How It Works (App Model)

  • Stadium-attending fans can scan their matchday tickets to create and host a Channel called "Fandom".
  • Each host can create only one channel per match, while home fans can follow unlimited channels.
  • Matches appear only once created by the Event Host.
  • Multiple sporting codes are supported.

User Categories

Fandom Hosts SuperFans Clubs / Teams Commercial Partners Ex-Professionals

Leaderboards & Rewards

Fans earn points through engagement such as posting, commenting, voting, quizzes, and match ups. Topic-based leaderboards (FanCharts) and regional rankings unlock rewards, discounts, and exclusive content.

Business Model

The app follows a freemium model. Free users access standard features while SuperFans unlock premium content such as Face Swaps, Fan Videos, Match Ups, and Celebration contests.

How to Get Started

Users can join as Channel Hosts, SuperFans, Clubs, or Sponsors. Once signed in, users can join fandoms, participate in challenges, and earn points.

Match Ups

Match Ups compare playlists, images, lineups, birthdays, or favorites. Fans rate each side using the fan rating system.
